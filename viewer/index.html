How to label maps
  1. Each card below is one map from your library. Click its picture to open the map and see what it shows.
  2. Places this map is in are tags: they say where the map is, so you can find it later. Start typing a state and pick it from the list. Add as many as apply.
  3. Footprint on the globe is the shape drawn for the map. Choose a rectangle around its places, its own rectangle, or none yet.
  4. Click Save at the top right. Maps with a footprint appear on the Globe tab.

Example: a county map in Wisconsin is tagged Wisconsin, but a statewide rectangle would be wrong for it. Choose "None yet", or enter its own rectangle.

Not sure about a map? Tick Come back to this one and find it later under the Come back to button. A title is optional; leave it empty to use the file name. The search box also finds maps by place.

Add maps to the atlas

  1. Drag map files, or a whole folder of them, into the box below. Folders inside folders are fine.
  2. Each map is copied into the atlas's library. Your original files are not changed or moved.
  3. New maps arrive with no places and no footprint. Go to Label maps to tag them yourself.
